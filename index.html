Frequently asked questions
How do I create an account?

Use Sign Up, enter a unique username, email and password. Confirm your email if confirmation is enabled.

Why can't I start a live stream?

Live streaming uses LiveKit. Sign in, allow camera and microphone access, then select Go Live. If a stream will not connect, check browser permissions and the streaming service configuration.

How do tournament registrations work?

Sign in and select Join Now on an open free tournament. Paid entry is not enabled.
